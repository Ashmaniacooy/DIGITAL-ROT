# DIGITAL-ROT
DIGITAL ROT — a visual archive of characters, aesthetics, corrupted memories, and experimental combinations exploring digital decay, liminal spaces, glitch culture, and fragmented reality.
DIGITAL ROT

A visual archive of characters, aesthetics, memories, and fragments of a reality that was never meant to be preserved.

DIGITAL ROT is an experimental visual and narrative project built around the collision of characters, aesthetics, distorted memories, digital decay, liminal spaces, and fragmented reality.

The project treats visual ideas as components of a constantly expanding archive — characters can interact with aesthetics, aesthetics can alter meaning, and combinations can create stories that do not necessarily reveal themselves at first glance.

PROJECT

DIGITAL ROT explores themes such as:

Digital decay
Glitch culture
Distorted memories
Liminal spaces
Dreamlike environments
Lost media
Webcore / Y2K aesthetics
Gothic and religious imagery
Organic and digital deterioration
Fragmented narratives
Experimental visual compositions

The goal is not simply to create images.

The goal is to create connections between them.

ARCHIVE

The project is built around several interconnected archives.

CHARACTER ARCHIVE

A collection of characters used as the primary narrative and visual elements of DIGITAL ROT.

Characters may exist independently or become part of larger visual combinations.

AESTHETIC ARCHIVE

A collection of visual aesthetics, moods, themes, and stylistic concepts that can be combined with characters and other elements.

ART STYLE ARCHIVE

A collection of rendering and illustration approaches used when a specific visual treatment strengthens a concept.

TEMPLATE ARCHIVE

Reusable visual compositions and structural templates for constructing new DIGITAL ROT pieces.

COMBINATIONS

The point where the archive becomes something more.

Characters, aesthetics, templates, art styles, and concepts are combined into individual visual experiments.

Every combination should have a purpose.

CORE PRINCIPLE

DIGITAL ROT is not a random collection of visual elements.

Every strong combination should contain:

Concept → Interaction → Mood → Visual Logic → Meaning

Elements should influence one another rather than simply coexist inside the same frame.

A character should not merely stand inside an aesthetic.

The aesthetic should change how the character is perceived.

A template should not merely hold the elements.

The composition should contribute to the idea.

VISUAL LANGUAGE

DIGITAL ROT frequently draws from:

GLITCH · DREAMCORE · WEBCORE · Y2K · LOST MEDIA · LIMINAL · GOTHIC · ANGELCORE · ANALOG HORROR · MEMORY DISTORTION

These are not strict boundaries.

The archive is designed to evolve.

STORYTELLING

Not every story in DIGITAL ROT is told directly.

Some pieces may contain:

hidden details
visual contradictions
recurring symbols
character quirks
fragmented memories
environmental clues
subtle connections between unrelated pieces

The viewer is not always supposed to receive the complete answer.

Sometimes the archive itself may not have one.
